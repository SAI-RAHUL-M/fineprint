How it works

  1. We split your lease into clauses

    Rent, deposit, repairs, entry, renewal. Each one gets a one-line summary in plain English.

  2. Two reviewers read every clause

    Two different open-source language models (OpenAI's gpt-oss and Google's Gemma) each judge every clause on their own, using a plain-language guide to common lease clauses that we wrote, searched through a DigitalOcean Knowledge Base. When both flag the same clause, you know it's worth asking.

  3. Nothing goes unsourced

    A third model checks every note against the guide it cites. If the guide doesn't back it up, the note gets softened and labelled.

Fineprint explains; it never tells you whether something is legal. For that, talk to UMD Off-Campus Housing or Student Legal Aid.
