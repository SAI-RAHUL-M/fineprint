RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Residential Lease Agreement ("Lease") is entered into on July 15, 2026, between Hollowbrook Property Group LLC ("Landlord") and Jordan Example and Casey Sample ("Tenants"), for the premises located at 1234 Example Avenue, Unit 3B, College Park, MD 20740 ("Premises"). This is a fictional sample lease for demonstration purposes only.

1. TERM. The Lease begins on August 20, 2026 and ends on August 15, 2027.

2. RENT. Tenants shall pay monthly rent of $2,100.00, due on the first (1st) day of each month, payable through the Landlord's online resident portal.

3. LATE FEES. Rent not received by 11:59 PM on the first (1st) day of the month is late. Tenants shall pay a late fee of $150.00 on the second day, plus an additional $25.00 for each day thereafter until rent is paid in full.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenants shall pay a security deposit of $2,100.00 prior to move-in. The deposit will be held by Landlord and applied according to this Lease.

5. CLEANING FEE. In addition to the security deposit, Tenants shall pay a non-refundable cleaning and carpet replacement fee of $600.00 at move-in, regardless of the condition of the Premises at move-out.

6. JOINT AND SEVERAL LIABILITY. Each Tenant is jointly and severally liable for all obligations under this Lease, including the full amount of rent, regardless of which Tenant occupies the Premises.

7. UTILITIES. Tenants are responsible for electricity and internet. Landlord provides water, sewer and trash service and will bill Tenants a flat monthly utility fee of $95.00.

8. USE AND OCCUPANCY. The Premises shall be used only as a private residence by the Tenants listed above. Guests may not stay more than fourteen (14) nights in any six-month period without Landlord's written consent.

9. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS. Tenants shall be responsible for all repairs to the Premises, including plumbing, heating, air conditioning, appliances and structural items, regardless of cause. Tenants shall hire licensed contractors at their own expense.

10. LANDLORD ENTRY. Landlord and its agents may enter the Premises at any time, with or without notice, for inspection, repairs, showings or any other purpose Landlord deems necessary.

11. ALTERATIONS. Tenants shall not paint, install fixtures, or make alterations to the Premises without prior written consent of Landlord. Small nail holes for hanging pictures are permitted.

12. PETS. No pets are permitted on the Premises without a separate signed pet addendum. Service and assistance animals are not considered pets.

13. SUBLETTING AND ASSIGNMENT. Tenants shall not sublet the Premises or assign this Lease without the prior written consent of Landlord, which shall not be unreasonably withheld. A subletting administrative fee of $250.00 applies to approved requests.

14. RENEWAL. This Lease shall automatically renew for an additional twelve (12) month term at a rent increase determined by Landlord, unless Tenants provide written notice of non-renewal at least one hundred twenty (120) days before the end of the term.

15. EARLY TERMINATION. If Tenants terminate this Lease early for any reason, all rent for the remainder of the term becomes immediately due, plus a lease-break fee equal to two (2) months' rent.

16. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY. Landlord shall not be responsible for any injury, loss or damage to persons or property occurring on or about the Premises from any cause whatsoever, including Landlord's own negligence.

17. RENTER'S INSURANCE. Tenants are encouraged to maintain renter's insurance covering their personal property and liability for the duration of this Lease.

18. SMOKING. Smoking of any substance is prohibited inside the Premises and on balconies.

19. MOVE-OUT. Tenants shall return all keys and access cards on or before the last day of the term and provide a forwarding address in writing.

20. NOTICES. Notices to Landlord shall be delivered in writing to the management office or through the online resident portal. Notices to Tenants may be delivered to the Premises or by email.

21. ENTIRE AGREEMENT. This Lease, together with any signed addenda, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and may only be modified in writing signed by both parties.
